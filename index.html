01 — About the Club

Two Traditions.
One Floor.

Step draws from military drill, stomp, and gumboot traditions — precise, percussive, built on rhythm you make with your own body. Swing pulls from Lindy Hop and jazz-age ballrooms — partner-led, playful, built on momentum.

We train both, every week, so members leave with a wider vocabulary of movement than any single-style club offers. Sessions are open to complete beginners and returning dancers alike — you don't need experience to register.

02 — What We Dance

The Repertoire

A quick look at what members train in across the semester. This is here for context only — your registration form doesn't ask you to pick one, so describe your own interests in your own words.

01

Step

Body percussion, stomps, claps and vocal calls — rhythm built entirely from the group, no music required.

02

Lindy Hop Swing

The original swing partner dance — six and eight-count patterns, breakaways, and social floor craft.

03

Contemporary Step

Modern fusion of step fundamentals with hip-hop and contemporary phrasing for stage routines.

04

Swing-Salsa Blend

Cross-training piece mixing swing footwork with Latin partner turns for competition sets.
